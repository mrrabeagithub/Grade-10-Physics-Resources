Pedagogical Physics Notes

1. Laws of Reflection:

  • The incident ray, normal, and reflected ray lie in the same plane.
  • Angle of Incidence = Angle of Reflection ($\hat{i} = \hat{r}$).

Diffuse Reflection Insight: Even on a rough surface, the law $\hat{i} = \hat{r}$ remains locally true for every photon. The scattering occurs solely because the local normal vectors ($\vec{n}$) vary across the microscopic bumps!
